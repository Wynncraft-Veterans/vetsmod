Wynncraft: https://wynncraft.com/

Wynncraft's art belongs to Wynncraft. /wv retro reads it from the resource pack the player's own
game downloaded from the server, while the game runs; none of its files are in vetsmod's jar.
vetsmod is a community project, not made or endorsed by Wynncraft.

vetsmod bundles two main-menu panoramas, each six screenshots its authors took in game:

- Old Detlas, a Wynncraft city, drawn in the textures /wv retro draws, Minecraft's and Vanilla
  Tweaks' among them, under src/client/resources/retro/vetsmod (retro/vetsmod in the jar), which
  /wv retro shows;
- Vetlas, drawn in Wynncraft's own textures, under
  src/main/resources/assets/vetsmod/textures/gui/title/background (the same path under assets/ in
  the jar), which an easter egg shows.

The screenshots are vetsmod's own. What they show stays its owners': Old Detlas and Wynncraft's
